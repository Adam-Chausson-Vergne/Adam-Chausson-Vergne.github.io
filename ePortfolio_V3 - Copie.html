Engineering Course and Projects

EEEA Engineering Program Overview

At ENSEEIHT (N7), I am enrolled in the EEEA department (Electronics, Electrical Energy, and Automation). This three-year multidisciplinary curriculum provides a comprehensive foundation in modern hardware and software engineering.

The program focuses on key engineering fields:

  • Electronics: Analog/digital circuit design, signal processing, and microelectronics.
  • Automation & Control Systems: Modeling of dynamic systems, feedback loops, and state estimation.
  • Computer Science & Embedded Hardware: Low-level C programming, Python scripting, and digital logic.
  • Electrical Energy: Energy conversion, power electronics, and electrical networks.

Through hands-on laboratory sessions and practical projects, I am developing a strong dual skill set in hardware design and embedded programming, preparing me directly for a career in embedded systems for the aeronautics industry.

Star Pattern Recognition Algorithm (TIPE)

During my preparatory classes, I carried out a supervised research project (TIPE) on star pattern recognition algorithms. I coded a Python software designed to identify constellations in night sky photographs by analyzing geometric star arrangements and comparing them with a database. This technology is used in satellite "star trackers" for orientation sensing. During the testing phase, I achieved a detection rate of 85% on simulated data and 67% on distorted real-life photos. This experience strengthened my coding abilities and problem-solving skills.
